How it plays

Simple rules.
Fair ones.

You compete only against your own records. There are no leaderboards, no sign-in and no pause button, so every best time is a real one.

  1. Earn your way up

    Easy and Medium are open from the start. Solve three puzzles at a level to unlock the next: Hard, then Expert, then Master.

  2. Lives and hints

    You start with five of each, shared across every level. Lives come back one every 20 minutes and hints one every hour, up to three of each.

  3. Resets when you need them

    A reset adds three lives and three hints, clears your wrong numbers and keeps your correct work. You start with two.

  4. Pencil & paper daily

    Each unlocked level has a fresh daily puzzle with no lives, hints or score. Just you and the grid.
