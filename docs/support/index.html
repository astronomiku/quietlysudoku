SUPPORT

How can we help?

For questions, feedback, or troubleshooting, email mastermindco25@gmail.com.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The game works offline. iCloud sync and purchases require a connection.

How do levels unlock?

Easy and Medium are open from the start. Solve three puzzles at the preceding level to open Hard, Expert, and Master one at a time.

Where is my progress?

Progress is saved on your device. When iCloud is available, your games, settings, records, lives, hints, and resets sync through your private iCloud database.

What does a reset do?

A reset adds three lives and three hints, clears wrong numbers, and keeps your correct work.
