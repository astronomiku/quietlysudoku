PRIVACY POLICY

Quiet by design.

Effective October 20, 2026

Quietly: Sudoku is developed by Michiko Yamamoto. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Gameplay information

The app stores puzzle progress, settings, records, lives, hints, resets, and daily-puzzle activity so it can save your game and provide its features.

On-device storage and iCloud

Gameplay information is stored locally on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is available, the app uses Apple CloudKit to sync gameplay information through the private iCloud database associated with your Apple Account. If iCloud is unavailable, the app continues to work and saves progress locally.

Purchases

Optional reset-pack purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. The app receives transaction information needed to verify and deliver purchased items.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The app contains no advertising and does not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. The developer does not sell personal information.

Sharing

The developer does not share gameplay information with advertisers or data brokers. Apple may process information when you use iCloud or make an App Store purchase, subject to Apple’s terms and privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains on your device until you remove the app or erase its data. Synced information may remain in your private iCloud storage until you remove it through your Apple Account’s iCloud storage controls.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children and does not include advertising, social features, or public profiles.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app’s features or legal requirements change. The effective date will be revised when material changes are published.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact Michiko Yamamoto at mastermindco25@gmail.com.
